Coverage matters.

October 2015 reached only 46.60% valid land coverage. A zero detected burned area under poor coverage is not evidence that no fires occurred.

Seasonal soil moisture and rainfall show strong negative associations with detected burned area. These relationships identify patterns, not causal mechanisms.
